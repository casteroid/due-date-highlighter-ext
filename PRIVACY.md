# Privacy policy

Due Date Highlighter does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data.

- **Page content.** On the sites you turn it on for, the extension reads the text of the page inside your browser to find dates and highlight them. The text never leaves your device.
- **Settings.** Your tier profiles, formats, keywords and site rules are stored with `chrome.storage.local` on your device. They are not synced and not sent anywhere. Export writes them to a file you choose.
- **Permissions.** The extension asks for access to a site only when you enable it there, and gives the access back when you remove or disable the rule.
- **Network.** The extension makes no network requests, loads no remote code, and contains no analytics or tracking.

Uninstalling the extension deletes its stored settings.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/nunofaria11/due-date-highlighter-ext/issues>.
